Pitch Awareness

An extremely useful ability for chanters is to hear a hymn being sung and know which notes are being sung (Νη or Δι or whatever they may be). This is essential to move beyond just copying what we hear, towards understanding what we hear and becoming confident solo chanters.

This interactive exercise will help you to improve your pitch awareness. Start with the smallest range of notes (just Νη and Πα), and only increase the difficulty when you are comfortable. Practice a little every day for best results!
