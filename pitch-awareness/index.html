Pitch Awareness

An extremely useful ability for chanters is to hear a melody and know which notes are being sung (Νη, Πα, Βου, etc.). This is essential for moving beyond just copying what we hear, towards understanding what we hear and becoming confident solo chanters.

This interactive exercise will help you to improve your pitch awareness. Start with the smallest range of notes (just Νη and Πα), and increase the difficulty only when you are comfortable. Practice a little every day for best results!
