Note recognition

An extremely useful ability for chanters is to hear a melody and know which notes are being sung (Νη, Πα, Βου, etc.). This is essential for moving beyond just copying what we hear, towards understanding what we hear and becoming confident solo chanters.

This interactive exercise will help you to improve your ability to recognise the differences between different notes in the diatonic scale. Start with the smallest range of notes (just Νη and Πα), and when you can score 90% or more consistently, add more notes. Practice a little every day for best results!
